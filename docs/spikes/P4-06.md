# P4-06: tuning similarity.separationMargin

SPEC §5.3: `cleanroom.similarity.separationMargin`, default 0.0, ⚠️ ASSUMPTION. Controls count as separated when `s_pos − s_neg > separationMargin`. cleanflate-bench used a strict `pos > neg`, which is a margin of 0.

Before: 0.0.
After: 0.0, unchanged. The smallest control gap on the calibration fixtures is 0.508, so any margin up to 0.5 gives the same bands there.

## Method

The calibration fixtures under `test/fixtures/similarity/` (P4-05) hold toy implementations of one small spec: `a` and `b` are independent Rust implementations (the negative control), and `b-derived` is `b` with about a third of its local identifiers renamed and its functions reordered (the positive control). `scorePair` ran with the defaults (`ngram` of 5 and 10, `minIdentLen` 6, `commentNgram` 5) and no exclusions. The gap is `s_pos − s_neg` for each metric.

| Metric | s_neg (a, b) | s_pos (b, b-derived) | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ngram_jaccard@5` | 0.292 | 0.972 | 0.681 |
| `ngram_containment@5` | 0.480 | 0.987 | 0.508 |
| `ngram_jaccard@10` | 0.158 | 0.929 | 0.771 |
| `ngram_containment@10` | 0.290 | 0.964 | 0.674 |
| `ident_jaccard` | 0.133 | 0.769 | 0.636 |
| `ident_match_rate_left` | 0.256 | 0.870 | 0.613 |
| `comment_jaccard` | 0.011 | 0.743 | 0.732 |

The smallest gap is 0.508 (`ngram_containment@5`), so that is the headroom: a margin would have to exceed it before it changed any band on these fixtures. No metric's gap is close to zero.

## What this does and does not show

The fixtures are built so the controls separate, which is what they are for: they show the scorer's arithmetic and the band edges behave, not what real prior art looks like. cleanflate-bench's own n-gram metrics did not separate its real controls (a cross-language positive control scored lower than the negative on token overlap), which is the case a margin is for. A margin above 0 would then call a thin positive gap "not separated" instead of positioning a test pair on a nearly flat scale. The fixtures cannot tell whether 0.05 or 0.1 is the right size for that, and picking one without a real pair would be a guess.

## Decision

`separationMargin` stays at 0.0. The first real contamination run is the measurement that can move it: when a control gap there is positive but small, record the gap and decide with the data in hand. This spike changes no code.
